You're set. 😊

Sanity is on. Instagram in this browser is now messages only.

  1. Open Instagram. You land on your messages.
  2. Anything else shows a calm black screen with a way back.
  3. Pin Sanity to your toolbar: click the puzzle icon, then the pin.

What you'll see

Stays visible

  • Direct messages and text, and story replies (the text)
  • Photos and videos friends send, including one-time ones
  • Voice notes and audio
  • Login and two-factor pages
  • YouTube, except Shorts
  • Your music and the controls (Spotify, YouTube Music)

Covered

  • Instagram feed, Reels, Explore, Stories, profiles
  • Reels, posts and stories shared in a chat
  • Profile pictures (a smiley instead)
  • TikTok, except messages
  • YouTube Shorts, wherever they appear
  • Music videos (the sound keeps playing) and all album pictures
  • Reels, Shorts and TikToks shared in WhatsApp, and in search results
  • Explicit searches on Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo
  • Anything Sanity doesn't recognise

Good to know

  • Shortcut: Alt+Shift+M opens your messages. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • Turning off takes 30 seconds, then your PIN. Turning on is instant.
  • Explicit searches use Chrome's built-in on-device AI, when it's installed. The search text never leaves your computer. If the AI isn't installed, Sanity can't judge a search and shows results as usual.
  • Privacy: Sanity never reads, stores or sends your messages or searches. Nothing leaves your browser.
  • Limits: desktop Chrome only. Phone apps and system notifications aren't covered. Sites change their layout often, so some covers may need an update.
