You're set. 😊

Sanity is on. Instagram in this browser is now messages only.

  1. Open Instagram. You land on your messages.
  2. Anything else shows a calm black screen with a way back.
  3. Pin Sanity to your toolbar: click the puzzle icon, then the pin.

What you'll see

Stays visible

  • Direct messages and text
  • Photos and videos friends send from their camera or gallery
  • Story replies (the text)
  • Login pages

Covered

  • Feed, Reels, Explore, Stories
  • Profiles, search, notifications
  • Reels, posts and stories shared in a chat
  • Anything Sanity doesn't recognise

Settings

 

Turn-off PIN

Turning Sanity off asks for this PIN. The first try always says "wrong" and asks if you're sure, so it's a deliberate step. It's a commitment, not a lock: anyone with access to this browser can still remove the extension.

Good to know

  • Shortcut: Alt+Shift+M opens your messages. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • Turning off takes 30 seconds, then your PIN. Turning on is instant.
  • Privacy: Sanity never reads, stores or sends your messages. Nothing leaves your browser.
  • Limits: desktop Chrome only. Phone apps and system notifications aren't covered.
